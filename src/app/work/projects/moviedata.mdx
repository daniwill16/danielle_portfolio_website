## Overview
Reelette is a movie discovery platform that makes exploring films more intuitive, social, and personalized. The application combines curated movie data with social features like feeds, ratings, and group chats so users can find films they’ll enjoy and see what others are watching and talking about.

The focus of the project is a clean, responsive UI and a social‑media‑inspired experience that makes movie browsing feel fun and community‑driven.

## Key Features
- **Personalized Discovery:**  
  Browse movies by genre, popularity, and user interest, with sections that highlight relevant and engaging titles.

- **Trending & Highlights:**  
  Dedicated areas for movies that are currently popular or frequently interacted with on the platform.

- **Interactive Social Feed:**  
  A feed where users can see posts, reactions, and shared thoughts about different movies.

- **Movie Ratings & Comments:**  
  Users can rate films and leave short reviews, contributing to a community‑driven sense of what’s worth watching.

- **Personal Library & Watchlist:**  
  Save movies of interest, organize what to watch next, and keep track of favorites.

- **Group Chats & Community Spaces:**  
  Spaces for collaborative discussions, movie nights, and shared recommendations.
