## Black Student Union

I was an active member of the **Black Student Union (BSU)** at my university, a student organization dedicated to supporting, empowering, and creating community for Black students on campus. Through BSU, I participated in cultural events, discussions, and initiatives that promoted representation, belonging, and student advocacy. Being part of this organization strengthened my connection to campus life and allowed me to contribute to a supportive and inclusive community.
