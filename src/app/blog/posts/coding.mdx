## Self-Taught Programming & Continuous Learning

Outside of my coursework at **Louisiana State University**, I have taken the initiative to independently learn new programming languages, frameworks, and tools on my own. Whether through online resources, tutorials, or hands-on personal projects, I am constantly pushing myself to expand my technical skill set beyond what is taught in the classroom. This self-driven approach to learning reflects my passion for technology and my commitment to becoming a well-rounded developer in the ever-evolving field of computer science.
